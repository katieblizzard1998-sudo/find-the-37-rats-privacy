Privacy Policy for Find All (37) Rats

Last updated November 20, 2026

Find All (37) Rats is an Android game published by Angel Pet Studios. This Privacy Policy explains how the game handles information and how Angel Pet Studios uses information you provide when contacting support.

Gameplay progress

The game saves which rats you have found in local storage on your device so you can resume playing. Angel Pet Studios does not receive or store a copy of your saved progress. The game does not currently offer account creation or sign-in.

Starting a new game replaces your saved progress. You can delete your saved progress by clearing the game’s storage in Android Settings.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to display banner ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as your IP address, device or account identifiers, interactions with the game or ads, and diagnostic information such as crash logs and performance data. Google may use this information to deliver and measure ads, perform analytics, and prevent fraud and abuse. Google may also use your IP address to estimate your approximate location.

Google’s data handling and retention practices are described in its Privacy Policy and its explanation of how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can manage your Google ad personalization settings through My Ad Center.

Device permissions

The game does not request Android permissions to access your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Support communications

If you email Angel Pet Studios, the studio receives your email address and any information you include in your message. Angel Pet Studios uses this information to respond to your inquiry and retains it for as long as needed to address the issue and meet applicable legal obligations. You can use the contact address below to request deletion of personal information you provided to Angel Pet Studios.

Changes to this policy

Angel Pet Studios will post any changes to this Privacy Policy on this page and update the date above.
