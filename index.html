Privacy Policy

Find All (37) Rats, published by Angel Pet Studios. Last updated November 20, 2026.

This policy describes how the game handles information. It applies to the Android app Find All (37) Rats.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. It does not ask you to create an account. It does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking, and it does not send information to Angel Pet Studios or to any other party.

Information stored on your device

The game remembers which rats you have found so your progress is still there when you open it again. That list is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. Playing the level again replaces it with a new game.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and the new version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the developer contact on the Google Play listing for Find All (37) Rats.
